Record maximum phonation time (MPT)

A helper times three sustained “ah” trials on one breath each. Review recordings and waveforms, rest between attempts, and save the longest of three confirmed times separately from voice play.

Observer timing has reaction error. This is not a clinically validated diagnostic tool and does not grade normality or recovery.
