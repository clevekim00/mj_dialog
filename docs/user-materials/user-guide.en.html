Use what helps you

Two tools for practice

Sound graph

It is on by default.
A large graph is also available in consonant and word practice.
Turn it off if you do not need it.

A larger wave does not mean better pronunciation. Microphone distance and background sound also affect the graph.

Mirror

Watch your mouth on the screen.

Camera permission is needed. This mirror does not save video. You can practice with it off.

Fly gently with your voice

Training → Comfortable voice practice
→ Gentle voice flight

① Select your fatigue level and start.
② Be quiet for a moment at the beginning.
③ Try a comfortable “ah” sound.

The bird moves when the app detects your voice.
Changes in pitch move it slightly up or down.

Stop and rest whenever you need to.

A stopped bird is not a failure

The app may not have detected your voice clearly.
You do not need to force a louder sound.

Each round lasts up to 20 seconds.
You do not have to make a sound for all 20 seconds.

The recorded time estimates a continuous stretch of sound detected by the app. It is not a maximum phonation time (MPT) test—the longest time you can sustain a voice sound on one breath.

If something does not work

Recording will not start

Check microphone permission for the app in your device settings.

The mirror will not open

Check camera permission.
If your device is not supported, practice without the mirror.

Saving failed

Follow the retry prompt before closing the app.

Measure maximum phonation time (MPT)

Training → Comfortable voice practice → Maximum phonation time (MPT)

A helper times your voice. This is separate from the flying game.

  1. Choose fatigue, rest, and prepare in a quiet place with your helper.
  2. Select Prepare recording. Take a full breath, then sustain a comfortable “ah” on one exhalation without straining.
  3. Your helper presses Voice onset · Start timer at the start of sound, and Voice offset · Stop timer at its end.
  4. Listen to the recording and check the waveform. Confirm only a correctly timed, single-breath attempt with no cough or interference. Exclude mistakes or another breath.
  5. Rest and repeat until 3 valid trials are confirmed. The longest time is your MPT record. Find it in Records → MPT measurement.

Stop for pain, dizziness or breathlessness. Interrupted or unreviewed attempts do not count. A recording reaching the 120-second memory cap is excluded. An incomplete set has no final MPT and cannot be resumed; start a new set if needed. Retry any save error before closing the app.

Observer reaction affects timing. This app is not clinically validated and gives no normal/abnormal grade or diagnosis. Ask a clinician to interpret the result.
