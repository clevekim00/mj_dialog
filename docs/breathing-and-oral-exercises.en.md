## Tongue exercises

Perform each item 20 times.

1. Extend the tongue as far as possible, then repeatedly move it up and down.
2. Touch the upper and lower lips with the tongue.
3. Alternately touch the left and right corners of the mouth.
4. Lick around the entire lips clockwise and counterclockwise.
5. Click the tongue at the soft-palate area, slowly and then quickly.
6. Touch the upper teeth, then sweep the tongue backward along the roof of the mouth.
7. Keep the mouth open and hold the tongue against the hard palate for a long time.
   - Open the mouth downward as far as possible.
8. Apply resistance with a tongue depressor while extending the tongue to strengthen it.
   - Directions: forward, left, and right.
9. Alternately place the tongue behind the upper and lower lips to make a monkey-like face.
10. Touch the left and right molars with the tongue.
11. Move along the upper and lower teeth as if checking each tooth individually.
12. Push alternately against both cheeks with the tongue, as if holding a sweet in the cheek.
13. Make a clock sound (`똑딱똑딱`, tick-tock).
14. Practice tongue clicks (`쯧쯧쯧`), as if calling a dog.

## Lip exercises

Perform each item 20 times.

1. Draw the lips inward, then extend them forward.
2. Alternate the mouth shapes for `/우/` and `/이/`.
3. Alternate the mouth shapes for `/아/` and `/이/`.
4. Alternate the mouth shapes for `/우/` and `/아/`.
5. Make a kissing shape about 10 times slowly, then about 10 times quickly.
6. Spread the lips to both sides, then make a kissing shape.
7. Open the mouth as wide as possible for about 2 seconds.
8. Repeatedly cover the upper lip with the lower lip, then the lower lip with the upper lip.
9. Grimace on one side of the face so the lips shift to one side.
10. Make a sound similar to `빠`, with a sense of closing and then releasing.
11. Make a fish-mouth shape.
12. Alternate a sad expression and a smiling expression.

## Alternating movements

Perform each item quickly, 20 times.

1. Repeat `우이우이`.
2. `바-빠-파-마`
3. `아이아`
4. `다-따-타-나`
5. `러러러`
6. `퍼-터-러-커`
7. `퍼퍼퍼……`
8. `터터터……`
9. `커커커……`
10. `퍼터커……`

## Breathing training

Perform each item 20 times.

1. While seated, inhale deeply and lean the chest backward.
2. Hold the breath and, when unable to hold it any longer, inhale forcefully.
3. Breathe deeply and quickly.
4. Imitate hiccupping and sobbing.
5. After inhaling, hold the breath as long as possible without immediately exhaling, then exhale slowly.
6. Inhale a small amount and maintain a breath hold.
7. Exhale a small amount and maintain a breath hold.
8. Inhale deeply, then sustain `/아/` for as long as possible.
9. Inhale deeply, then sustain `/이/` for as long as possible.
10. Inhale deeply, then sustain `/우/` for as long as possible.
