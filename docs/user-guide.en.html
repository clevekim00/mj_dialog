02 CONSONANT PRACTICE / CHOOSE

Choose one consonant to practice.

Home › Choose a consonant to practice › Choose a consonant

  1. Initial or Final

    Choose the sound position.

  2. Tap the consonant you want.

    We will use ㄱ for this example.

  3. Choose a stage and repeat goal.

    Syllable · Word · Short sentence

04 CONSONANT PRACTICE / LISTEN AGAIN

Listen to your own voice.

  1. Listen to this recording

    Hear what you just said.

  2. Choose whether to try again.

    Choose Practice the same item again or Next item.

  3. Finish when you reach your goal.

    Tap Finish and return.

If you recorded the same item and consonant before,
Listen to previous recording of this item is also available.
The practice language must also match.

Changing stages starts a new count.
Switching from Syllable to Word resets the count to 0.

3, 5, and 10 refer to saved recordings.How to rest →

05 REST AND RESUME

You can rest before you finish.

You do not have to reach the whole goal.

  1. If recording, finish it first.

    Tap Stop recording and save.

  2. Go back after saving.

    Tap the back arrow at the top.

  3. Continue next time.

    On Home, look for Resume.

Stop if you feel uncomfortable.
Follow the plan agreed with your professional, if you have one.

You now know how consonant practice works.Try a sentence →

09 WHEN THINGS GO WRONG

The app may misunderstand you.

‘Analysis unavailable’ does not mean your pronunciation is wrong.

The microphone will not turn on.

Check this app's microphone permission in device settings. Ask someone helping you if needed.

The app shows different words.

Listen to your recording first. Retry when comfortable, or rest.

I cannot hear the example.

Check volume and headphones. You can also practice from the text.

There is no score.

Free conversation has no pronunciation score. Other practice also leaves scores empty when comparison information is unavailable.

‘Text match’ compares written text.

It shows how closely the recognized words match the target text.
It is not pronunciation accuracy or treatment effectiveness.

You do not have to do everything well at once.About your recordings →

10 ABOUT CONSONANT RECORDINGS

Server analysis is optional.

Consonant recordings are saved on your device first.
You can listen and compare without server analysis.

You can skip this for now.
Knowing how to record and listen again is enough to practice consonants.

If you want server analysis

After recording a consonant, open Optional: analyze phoneme intervals on the server. Check the destination and data, consent, then choose Send the consented recording to the server. Your recording and information such as the practice sentence are sent. A connected analysis server is required. While waiting, you can choose Cancel analysis.
